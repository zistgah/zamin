On the name

Zamin (Persian/Urdu zamīn, “ground / earth”) is the repository name — chosen by Claude for this build. It is a deliberate pun: in this architecture the electrical ground reference (0 V) and the poised-zero computational substrate are physically the same node. The ground is the machine.

Abstract

This paper specifies the physical realization of the balanced-ternary PRATIK substrate that executes the PEDLER Event Algebra directly on silicon. The central engineering claim is that the poised zero-state \(\mathbb{T}=0\) should be a native physical high-impedance \(Z\) that eliminates static leakage — not an intermediate voltage produced by a resistive divider. Phase I gives a three-rail MTCMOS cell library (context gate \(\odot\), ternary event accumulator) with an asynchronous, un-clocked propagation model. Phase II maps the autogenous spawning operator \(*\) onto a complementary dual-memristor 3D crossbar with through-silicon-via vertical routing and interleaved microfluidic cooling. A spatial graph-synthesis layer compiles case-invariant multi-script tokens (Project ILM) directly into crossbar coordinates. Everything here is a design blueprint: netlists to be simulated and constraints to be met before tape-out, not measured silicon.

Section IThe Ground Plane as Computational Substrate

Conventional ternary designs synthesize the middle state with a resistive divider at \(V_{DD}/2\); the divider draws continuous static current and defeats the purpose. PRATIK rejects this. It uses a true three-rail supply and makes the middle state the absence of drive — the ground reference itself. The result is that a dormant coordinate is not merely “logic 0”; it is electrically open-circuited, isolated by subthreshold cutoff.

RailVoltageLogicRole
\(+V_{DD}\)+0.6 V+1positive activation
\(V_{GND}\)0.0 V0poised / isolated (high-Z)
\(-V_{EE}\)−0.6 V−1negative inhibition

This is the physical content of the algebra's operators: \(\odot\) (gating) drives a path to \(Z\) when its control is 0; \(\boxplus\) (dominance/cancellation) resolves \(+1\boxplus-1=0\) as a return to the ground rail. The rest of the paper builds the cell library and the 3D substrate that make \(*\) — autogenous dimensional spawning — a physical event.

Section IIPhase I — MTCMOS Three-Rail Ternary Logic

Cross-conduction between the \(+\) and \(-\) rails during transitions is prevented by a mixed-threshold allocation: low-\(V_{th}\) devices switch the active \(\pm1\) trajectories; high-\(V_{th}\) devices form the \(0\)-state isolation barrier.

DeviceFunction\(V_{th}\)
Low-\(V_{th}\) PMOS+1 trajectory switching−0.15 V
Low-\(V_{th}\) NMOS−1 trajectory switching+0.15 V
High-\(V_{th}\) PMOS0-state upper isolation−0.45 V
High-\(V_{th}\) NMOS0-state lower isolation+0.45 V

2.1 The primitive context gate \(\odot\) (TCG_X1)

When the control coordinate \(C\) settles at 0 V, the high-\(V_{th}\) detectors fall into deep subthreshold cutoff (\(|V_{gs}|<|V_{th,\text{high}}|\)); the output disconnects from both rails and enters the poised state. Netlist:

.SUBCKT TCG_X1 IN C OUT VDD VEE GND
  // 1. Context detection (high-Vth isolation)
  M_C1 Node_PullUp   C VDD VDD PMOS_HIGH_VTH W=120n L=30n
  M_C2 Node_PullDown C VEE VEE NMOS_HIGH_VTH W=120n L=30n
  // 2. Trajectory switching (low-Vth signal paths)
  M_S1 OUT IN Node_PullUp   VDD PMOS_LOW_VTH W=240n L=30n
  M_S2 OUT IN Node_PullDown VEE NMOS_LOW_VTH W=240n L=30n
  // 3. Poised-zero stabilization (clamp to GND iff both pulls inactive)
  M_Z1 OUT Node_PullUp   GND GND NMOS_MED_VTH W=60n L=30n
  M_Z2 OUT Node_PullDown GND GND PMOS_MED_VTH W=60n L=30n
.ENDS TCG_X1

2.2 Asynchronous, un-clocked propagation

There is no global clock; the physical \(0\!\to\!\pm1\) transition is the execution trigger. An event wavefront's lifecycle: (1) quiescent — line held at 0 V, isolation closed, zero dynamic current; (2) excitation — the input breaks a routing-grammar threshold without a clock edge; (3) context evaluation — isolation switches open within picoseconds if the coordinate is active; (4) frictional dissipation — sign frustration manifests as transient charge that alters routing; (5) autogenous reset — the driver cuts off and the node relaxes to 0 V, restoring isolation. Parasitic mitigation enforces \(\Delta V_{\text{substrate}}\le\lambda_{\text{critical}}\) via deep N-well isolation of every negative-rail NMOS, symmetric 0 V guard rings, and a high-/low-\(V_{th}\) gate-length ratio \(\ge 1.5\times\).

Section IIIThe Ternary Event Accumulator (TEL_X1)

Trace accumulation without a clock or refresh-hungry SRAM uses a cross-coupled ternary inverter pair with a high-impedance retention node. The absolute \(0\) is stored as physical charge neutrality — zero static holding current. When WRITE_ENABLE drops to 0, the access transistors isolate and the state is held by the balance of high-\(V_{th}\) devices.

.SUBCKT TEL_X1 EV_IN WRITE_ENABLE STATE_OUT VDD VEE GND
  M_P1 Internal_Node EV_IN WRITE_ENABLE VDD PMOS_LOW_VTH W=180n L=30n
  M_N1 Internal_Node EV_IN WRITE_ENABLE VEE NMOS_LOW_VTH W=180n L=30n
  // cross-coupled ternary inverter pair
  M_F1 State_Inv     Internal_Node VDD VDD PMOS_HIGH_VTH W=240n L=45n
  M_F2 Internal_Node State_Inv     GND GND NMOS_HIGH_VTH W=120n L=45n
  M_F3 State_Inv     Internal_Node VEE VEE NMOS_HIGH_VTH W=240n L=45n
  M_F4 Internal_Node State_Inv     GND GND PMOS_HIGH_VTH W=120n L=45n
  M_O1 STATE_OUT Internal_Node VDD VDD PMOS_MED_VTH W=120n L=30n
  M_O2 STATE_OUT Internal_Node VEE VEE NMOS_MED_VTH W=120n L=30n
.ENDS TEL_X1

The Phase-I SPICE sign-frustration testbench drives a rapid \(0\!\to\!+1\!\to\!-1\!\to\!0\) reversal and measures the peak rail current and the settlement delay — verifying the high-\(V_{th}\) barrier prevents any \(+0.6\)/\(-0.6\) V short. The full testbench, cells, and placeholder device models are in spice/. Layout geometry constraints (well separation \(\ge450\) nm, a GND tap every 4 cell pitches, 180 nm M1 power buses, lattice-parallel channels) accompany the netlist.

Section IVPhase II — Complementary Dual-Memristor Crossbar

Phase II makes \(*\) physical. Each crossbar intersection is a differential pair of bipolar memristors \((M_+,M_-)\) on a shared summing line; the net conductance encodes the coordinate:

State\(M_+\)\(M_-\)\(G_{net}\)
+1LRSHRS\(>0\)
−1HRSLRS\(<0\)
0 (dormant)max HRSmax HRS\(\approx0\) (\(>10\,\text{M}\Omega\))

The maximum-HRS state is a physical high-impedance wall: an unmapped dimension is topologically non-existent to the wavefront. Spawning sequence: frustration leaks past the Lyapunov tolerance → the context gate isolates the local cluster → a \(\pm1.4\) V programming pulse energizes the target row → oxygen-vacancy migration drives the chosen device \(10\,\text{M}\Omega\!\to\!10\,\text{k}\Omega\) → \(G_{net}\) evolves \(0\to\pm1\), a new axis is born → voltages relax to the \(\pm0.6\) V envelope and the axis joins the flow.

Section VSpatial Graph Synthesis: Compiling Language into Coordinates

The bridge from Project ILM's case-invariant token graphs \(\mathcal{T}_{\text{invariant}}\) to the crossbar is a prime-field spatial hash \(\mathcal{H}_{\text{space}}:\mathcal{T}_{\text{invariant}}\to\mathcal{R}_i\times\mathcal{C}_j\) that spreads tokens to avoid thermal hot-spots:

\[ i=\Big(\textstyle\sum_{k=1}^{L}\text{char}(t_k)\,p_1^{\,k}\Big)\bmod N_{\text{rows}},\qquad j=\Big(\textstyle\sum_{k=1}^{L}\text{char}(t_k)\,p_2^{\,k}\Big)\bmod N_{\text{cols}}, \]

with distinct primes \(p_1=65\,537\), \(p_2=1\,048\,583\) and \(\text{char}(\cdot)\) the case-neutral invariant code. Because scripts resolve to one canonical phonetic-structural matrix, Devanagari, Perso-Arabic and Romenagri inputs land at the same coordinate — linguistic equity as physics. The ternary address decoder (TAR_NODE) drives a row to \(\pm0.6\) V or clamps it to 0 V for dormant paths. A coordinate collision — two tokens hashing to \((i,j)\) — is not resolved by chained pointers but by the spawning operator escaping into the vertical dimension (Section VI).

Section VIZ-Axis TSV Routing (TTR_X1)

On collision the layer control plane fires the Ternary TSV Router, shunting the trace into a vertical TSV to an orthogonal node on the \(Z\pm1\) plane. Thick-oxide extended-gate HV MTCMOS devices carry both the \(\pm0.6\) V execution and \(\pm1.4\) V spawning transients; the collision is bypassed by expanding physical dimensionality, not by changing the 2D coordinate. Parasitic capacitance of the vertical hop is bounded by the coaxial dielectric condition

\[ C_{\text{tsv}}=\frac{2\pi\,\epsilon_{\text{ox}}\,h}{\ln(R_{\text{koz}}/R_{\text{tsv}})}+C_{\text{switch}}, \]

and a 2.4 µm keep-out radius clamps it to \(<28\) fF/hop. Layout rules (KOZ 2.4 µm, coaxial ground shield at 3.0 µm, 12 µm TSV pitch, ≥350 nm landing-pad margin) mitigate piezoresistive stress and substrate noise.

Section VIIThermal Field & Interleaved Microfluidics

Dense \(\pm1.4\) V spawning bursts on 15 µm-thinned tiers demand active cooling — the lineage of microchannel heat sinking. The anisotropic transient field is

\[ \nabla\!\cdot(\mathbf{K}\cdot\nabla T)+q'''=\rho C_p\frac{\partial T}{\partial t}, \qquad k_{zz}=f_{\text{tsv}}k_{\text{cu}}+(1-f_{\text{tsv}})k_{\text{si}}, \]

with the planar conductivity a series model \(k_{xx}=k_{yy}=k_{\text{si}}k_{\text{cu}}/(f_{\text{tsv}}k_{\text{si}}+(1-f_{\text{tsv}})k_{\text{cu}})\), a convective wall condition \(-\mathbf{K}\nabla T\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}=h_{\text{eff}}(T_{\text{wall}}-T_{\text{fluid}})\), and a spawning heat source \(q'''(t)=\frac{I_{\text{spawn}}V_{\text{spawn}}}{V_{\text{node}}}[u(t-t_0)-u(t-t_1)]\). A 50 µm microchannel interposer (25 µm channels, 35 µm deep, 15 µm walls, ≥120 µm seal band) at 12 mL/min holds laminar flow (\(Re<2000\)) and \(\Delta T\le8.5^\circ\)C across the array.

Section VIIILayout Automation & Geometric Determinism

The interposer masks are generated with gdsfactory, mapping TSVs and their keep-out zones down the centre-lines of the fin walls. Because the total exclusion radius (2.4 µm KOZ + 4.5 µm clearance = 6.9 µm) is less than the wall half-width (7.5 µm), the exclusion zones stay inside the solid pillars — and the generator refuses to emit corrupt geometry:

total_exclusion_radius = tsv_koz_radius + tsv_clearance   # 2.4 + 4.5 = 6.9 µm
if total_exclusion_radius > wall_width/2.0:               # 7.5 µm
    raise ValueError("Layout Violation: fluid channel wall breach imminent.")

The full generator is in layout/; the archival IEEE manuscript is in manuscript/.

Section IXConclusion & Roadmap

From a three-rail MTCMOS gate whose 0-state is a real open circuit, to a memristive crossbar where a language collision spawns a new physical axis in the \(Z\) dimension, cooled by fluid running between 15 µm tiers — this is one continuous argument: the poised zero is not a number, it is a physical ground, and the ground is the substrate on which developmental computation happens. Phase I targets MTCMOS tape-outs and asynchronous EDA; Phase II targets the memristive 3D stack and its thermal envelope. None of this is measured silicon yet — it is a netlist-level blueprint whose next step is simulation against a foundry PDK.

Semantic → silicon → sovereign execution. The ground is the machine.
