  primitive cells; poised-0 shown as a high-impedance dashed line; four presets.

  model; sign-frustration transient testbench; placeholder 30 nm MTCMOS models.
- gdsfactory microfluidic interposer generator (layout/) with a geometric-safety guard.
- IEEE-style manuscript (manuscript/pratik_phase2.tex) and verified references.bib.
- Select-text → "Ask AI" seeding; GPLv3 project scaffolding, CONTRACT.md, CONTEXT.md.
